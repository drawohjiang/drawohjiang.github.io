What we clean

Every room. Every surface. Every time.

Our room-by-room checklist means nothing gets skipped. The same thorough standard on every visit.

🛋️

Living Areas

Dusted, vacuumed, and wiped down from ceiling fans to baseboards — so your home feels settled and peaceful.

  • Dust all surfaces, shelves & décor
  • Vacuum upholstered furniture
  • Wipe windowsills & ledges
  • Sweep, vacuum & mop floors
  • Empty trash and replace liner
🍳

Kitchen

Degreased, sanitized, and sparkling — including all appliance exteriors. The heart of the home, at its best.

  • Clean countertops & backsplash
  • Wipe exterior of all appliances
  • Clean stovetop & drip pans
  • Scrub sink & faucets
  • Clean cabinet door fronts
  • Sweep & mop floor
🛁

Bathrooms

Full scrub and disinfect on every surface. The room your guests notice most — and the one we take most seriously.

  • Scrub toilet inside & out
  • Clean shower, tub & tile
  • Wipe mirror & vanity
  • Polish faucets & fixtures
  • Scrub sink & drain area
  • Mop floor, wipe baseboards
🛏️

Bedrooms

Dusted, vacuumed, and made — so you come home to a bedroom that feels calm, considered, and completely clean.

  • Make bed & straighten linens
  • Dust furniture, lamps & ceiling fans
  • Wipe nightstands & dressers
  • Vacuum carpet or mop hardwood
  • Empty trash

Questions

Frequently asked

Can I cancel or pause my membership?+
Yes. Cancel anytime with 30 days written notice. There are no cancellation fees. Pausing for up to 30 days is available once per year for Premium and Regular members.
Are your cleaners employees or contractors?+
All Peak Cleaning staff are fully trained to our standards and background-checked before their first job.
Do I need to be home during the clean?+
No — most clients are out when we arrive. You can provide a key or access code at booking. We'll text you when we're on the way and again when we're done.
What if I'm not satisfied with a clean?+
Text us within 24 hours and we'll come back to re-clean the area at no charge. No questions asked. Your satisfaction is the standard every visit is measured against.
How does the referral program work?+
After your first clean you'll receive a personal referral code by text. Share it with a friend — when they book a recurring plan, you both get $25 off your next clean. Refer 3 in the same month and your next clean is completely free.
What products do you use?+
We use professional-grade, pH-neutral cleaners that are safe for stone countertops and hardwood floors. Let us know at booking if you have product preferences or sensitivities and we'll accommodate them.
